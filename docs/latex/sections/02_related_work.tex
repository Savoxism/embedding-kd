\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Text Embedding Distillation}

%general KD --> embedding-specific KD --> pointwise/representation alignment --> relational/geometry-preserving KD.

Knowledge distillation transfers behavior from a large teacher to a compact student~\citep{hinton2015distilling}. Language-model compression commonly matches output distributions, hidden states, or attention maps~\citep{sanh2019distilbert,jiao2019tinybert,wang2020minilm,xu2024survey}, whereas embedding-specific methods target quantities used by similarity-based applications. Sparse Logit Sampling shows that deterministic top-$K$ teacher logits induce truncation bias and uses randomized teacher-logit sampling to preserve the full teacher distribution in expectation~\citep{anshumann2025sparse}. In that setting, however, the student still produces a full vocabulary vector; in corpus-level embedding KD, scoring an additional relation requires encoding another text, so support selection controls both approximation and encoder cost.

DistilCSE combines teacher supervision with contrastive learning~\citep{gao2021distilcse,xu2023distillcse}; EmbedDistill transfers relative query--document geometry~\citep{kim2023embeddistill}; and recent systems align embeddings, layers, tokens, or heterogeneous representation spaces~\citep{lee2024gecko,zhang2024jasperstella,vujanic2025leaf,truong2025emo,dao2026talas,akram2026jinaembeddingsv5}. Relational approaches match pairwise similarities, distances and angles, conditional affinities, graph edges, or topological summaries~\citep{tung2019similarity,park2019rkd,passalis2020pkt,liu2019irg,kim2024topkd}. Full Kernel Matrix Transfer uses Nystrom landmarks~\citep{qian2022fullkernel}, and BINGO groups teacher-similar instances~\citep{xu2022bingo}. Nevertheless, many relational objectives instantiate their relations from the current mini-batch; NRKD likewise mines an in-batch similarity matrix~\citep{xin2025nrkd}. This couples computational batching with geometric support selection.

\subsection{Beyond Mini-batch Relational Supervision}

%Relational KD is often instantiated within mini-batches --> prior works recognize that batch-local relations provide limited coverage --> some extend supervision beyond batches
Neighborhood graphs recover manifold structure in Laplacian Eigenmaps and
Diffusion Maps, where a row-stochastic transition matrix over a $k$NN graph
serves as the primitive description of local geometry~\citep{belkin2003laplacian,coifman2006diffusionmaps};
heat-based constructions summarize the same operator at multiple
scales~\citep{sun2009hks,huguet2023heatgeo}. Geometric KD aligns heat kernels
between teacher and student graphs~\citep{yang2022geometrickd}, and graph-to-MLP
methods distill observed graph structure into graphless
models~\citep{zhang2022glnn,yang2023graphkd_survey}; both assume a graph given by
the task. GGPKD instead constructs the graph from cached text embeddings, so the
teacher's own retrieval behavior defines which relations a standard text encoder
is supervised on. We use the transition operator at a single hop rather than its
diffused powers: the one-hop rows already provide a teacher-weighted support and
a per-row bandwidth, and multi-hop targets are treated as an alternative the
method does not require. Neither the graph nor the teacher is needed at
inference.
