\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Text embedding distillation.}
Knowledge distillation transfers the behavior of a large teacher to a compact student~\citep{hinton2015distilling}. Language-model compression commonly matches output distributions, hidden states, or attention maps~\citep{sanh2019distilbert,jiao2019tinybert,wang2020minilm,xu2024survey}. Sentence- and retrieval-embedding methods instead distill the quantities used for similarity search. DistilCSE variants combine teacher supervision with contrastive objectives~\citep{gao2021distilcse,xu2023distillcse}; EmbedDistill transfers relative query--document geometry and augments sparse regions of the data manifold~\citep{kim2023embeddistill}; and recent systems explore embedding-, layer-, or token-level transfer across heterogeneous encoders~\citep{lee2024gecko,zhang2024jasperstella,vujanic2025leaf,truong2025emo,dao2026talas,akram2026jinaembeddingsv5}. These methods establish strong embedding-specific transfer mechanisms, but do not treat anchor-conditioned corpus topology as the primary supervision object.

\paragraph{Geometry as distilled knowledge.}
A broad line of work supports the premise that a teacher's organization of examples is itself transferable knowledge. Similarity-preserving KD matches pairwise activation similarities~\citep{tung2019similarity}; RKD matches inter-example distances and angles~\citep{park2019rkd}; and PKT aligns conditional affinity distributions~\citep{passalis2020pkt}. Instance Relationship Graph distillation represents features as vertices and inter-instance relations as edges~\citep{liu2019irg}, while BINGO forms teacher-guided bags of related instances~\citep{xu2022bingo}. Most directly, TopKD defines global topology through persistence diagrams that summarize the shape, connectivity, and multi-scale structure of a latent point cloud~\citep{kim2024topkd}. Together, these results motivate geometry preservation as a distillation goal. RIPPLE specializes this goal to text encoders by defining geometry through anchor-wise teacher neighborhoods and their diffusion profiles over a fixed corpus.

\paragraph{The mini-batch relation gap.}
The existence of a relational loss does not ensure that it observes the relations that define corpus geometry. Similarity-preserving KD, for example, constructs its pairwise matrix from the current mini-batch~\citep{tung2019similarity}, and NRKD searches for neighbors within the mini-batch similarity matrix~\citep{xin2025nrkd}. Teacher-guided grouping enlarges the relational context~\citep{xu2022bingo}, whereas a global topological summary does not specify which corpus neighbors supervise each anchor~\citep{kim2024topkd}. We study this unresolved support question by making relational supervision anchor-specific and corpus-defined.

\paragraph{Graph geometry and diffusion.}
Neighborhood graphs have long been used to recover manifold structure, from Laplacian Eigenmaps to Diffusion Maps~\citep{belkin2003laplacian,coifman2006diffusionmaps}. Heat-based constructions further provide multi-scale descriptions of geodesic geometry~\citep{sun2009hks,huguet2023heatgeo}. In graph learning, geometric KD aligns neural heat kernels between teacher and student graphs~\citep{yang2022geometrickd}, and other graph-distillation methods transfer observed graph structure to graph models or graphless students~\citep{yang2023graphkd_survey,zhang2022glnn}. RIPPLE differs in both setting and deployment: its graph is induced from cached text embeddings solely to define supervision, while the student remains a standard text encoder that requires neither corpus graph nor teacher at inference.
