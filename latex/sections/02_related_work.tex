\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Text Embedding and Relational Distillation}
Knowledge distillation transfers behavior from a large teacher to a compact student~\citep{hinton2015distilling}. Language-model compression commonly matches output distributions, hidden states, or attention maps~\citep{sanh2019distilbert,jiao2019tinybert,wang2020minilm,xu2024survey}, whereas embedding-specific methods target quantities used by similarity-based applications. DistilCSE combines teacher supervision with contrastive learning~\citep{gao2021distilcse,xu2023distillcse}; EmbedDistill transfers relative query--document geometry~\citep{kim2023embeddistill}; and recent systems align embeddings, layers, tokens, or heterogeneous representation spaces~\citep{lee2024gecko,zhang2024jasperstella,vujanic2025leaf,truong2025emo,dao2026talas,akram2026jinaembeddingsv5}. Relational approaches match pairwise similarities, distances and angles, conditional affinities, graph edges, or topological summaries~\citep{tung2019similarity,park2019rkd,passalis2020pkt,liu2019irg,kim2024topkd}. Full Kernel Matrix Transfer uses Nystrom landmarks~\citep{qian2022fullkernel}, and BINGO groups teacher-similar instances~\citep{xu2022bingo}. Nevertheless, many relational objectives instantiate their relations from the current mini-batch; NRKD likewise mines an in-batch similarity matrix~\citep{xin2025nrkd}. This couples computational batching with geometric support selection.

\subsection{Graph Geometry and Diffusion}
Neighborhood graphs recover manifold structure in Laplacian Eigenmaps and Diffusion Maps~\citep{belkin2003laplacian,coifman2006diffusionmaps}; heat-based constructions provide multi-scale summaries~\citep{sun2009hks,huguet2023heatgeo}. Geometric KD aligns heat kernels between teacher and student graphs~\citep{yang2022geometrickd}, while graph-to-MLP methods distill observed graph structure into graphless models~\citep{zhang2022glnn,yang2023graphkd_survey}. RIPPLE instead induces a graph from cached text embeddings solely to select and diffuse supervision for a standard text encoder; neither graph nor teacher is used at inference.
