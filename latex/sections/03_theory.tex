\section{Global Geometry and the Mini-Batch Relation Gap}
\label{sec:theory}

\paragraph{Global geometry alignment.}
Let $\mathcal D=\{x_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$ be an unlabeled corpus. The teacher produces unit-normalized embeddings $t_i$ and cosine scores $c^T_{ij}=\cos(t_i,t_j)$; the student analogously produces $s_i$ and $c^S_{ij}$. We use \emph{global geometry} to refer to teacher-defined relations over $\mathcal D$, including the immediate teacher neighborhood
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:global-neighborhood}
\mathcal N_k^T(i)
=\operatorname{TopK}_{j\in\mathcal D\setminus\{i\}} c^T_{ij}
\end{equation}
and the multi-hop organization of these neighborhoods. The goal is to align this corpus-level structure between teacher and student.

\paragraph{Batch-local relational KD.}
For a mini-batch $\mathcal B\ni i$, let $\mathcal L_{\mathcal B}(i)=\mathcal B\setminus\{i\}$ denote the available comparison support. A representative relational objective compares
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:batchrel}
p_i^{T,\mathcal B}(j)\propto e^{c^T_{ij}/\tau_0},\qquad
p_i^{S,\mathcal B}(j)\propto e^{c^S_{ij}/\tau_0},
\qquad j\in\mathcal L_{\mathcal B}(i),
\end{equation}
through $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_i^{T,\mathcal B}\|p_i^{S,\mathcal B})$. Although the target is global geometry, the support of this loss is determined by the data loader and is independent of $\mathcal N_k^T(i)$.

\begin{proposition}[Batch-local support blindness]
\label{prop:coverage}
Assume that each epoch partitions the corpus uniformly into batches of size $B$. For an anchor $i$, let
\begin{equation}
\mathcal O_i^{(T)}=\bigcup_{e=1}^{T}\mathcal L_{\mathcal B_e}(i)
\end{equation}
be the examples compared with $i$ during $T$ independently reshuffled epochs. Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:coverage-exact}
\mathbb E\!\left[
\frac{|\mathcal O_i^{(T)}\cap\mathcal N_k^T(i)|}{k}
\right]
=q_T
=1-\left(1-\frac{B-1}{N-1}\right)^T
\leq\frac{T(B-1)}{N-1}.
\end{equation}
Moreover, the realized batch-relational objective depends on the $i$-th student score row only through $\{c^S_{ij}:j\in\mathcal O_i^{(T)}\}$. Scores $c^S_{ij}$ with $j\notin\mathcal O_i^{(T)}$ are therefore unidentified by that empirical objective.
\end{proposition}

Proposition~\ref{prop:coverage} does not imply that every unseen relation must be wrong. It shows that the batch-relational objective cannot determine most teacher-neighbor relations from its realized score arguments; Figure~\ref{fig:motivation} shows that this missing support leads to measurable distortion in practice. The result gives a direct design principle: \emph{teacher geometry should select relational supervision, while the mini-batch should serve only as a computational unit}. A full-corpus objective satisfies this requirement but is impractical to evaluate directly; Section~\ref{sec:method} develops a sparse approximation.
