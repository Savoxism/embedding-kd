\section{Analysis}
\label{sec:analysis}

The analysis follows the method's causal chain: whether teacher-defined locality improves the support, whether diffusion transfers structure beyond one hop, and how much sparse realization costs. We interpret downstream scores together with full-corpus relational diagnostics rather than using task performance alone as evidence for a mechanism.

\subsection{Does Teacher-Defined Locality Matter?}

Our primary matched-compute comparison is
\begin{equation}
\text{batch-local support}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{random corpus support}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{mutual-$k$NN support}.
\end{equation}
The first transition asks whether broader corpus exposure alone is sufficient; the second asks whether the teacher's local geometry matters when the number of anchor--column scores is held fixed. We report downstream average, full-corpus JS divergence, and retained teacher mass. All variants share the student initialization, optimizer, schedule, seeds, and scored-pair budget; Appendix~\ref{app:ablation-protocol} specifies the controls.

\subsection{Does Diffusion Recover Broader Geometry?}

We compare one-hop RIPPLE with the same model augmented by diffusion targets. The main evidence is teacher--student error on held-out broader-rank relations, full-corpus relational divergence, and downstream performance. Improvement outside the immediate mutual-$k$NN support would show that diffusion transfers broader geometry rather than merely reweighting direct neighbors.

Cross-anchor row supervision appears as an additional row in the component ablation rather than a separate main-paper study. The fuller controls---including injection-only supervision, graph-width sweeps, fixed rank bands, row-overlap disagreement, and a degree-preserving random graph---are reported in Appendix~\ref{app:additional-analysis}.

\subsection{Accuracy--Efficiency Trade-off}

We vary the candidate budget and jointly report retained teacher mass, downstream average, full-corpus JS divergence, step time, and peak memory. This reveals whether the local targets saturate before the cost approaches full-corpus scoring. The same sweep also tracks the two terms in Proposition~\ref{prop:global-geometry}: selected-support distortion $\bar\epsilon$ and missed teacher mass $\bar\delta$.
