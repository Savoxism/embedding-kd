\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\paragraph{Benchmarks and metrics.}
Following the evaluation protocol used in TALAS~\citep{dao2026talas}, we evaluate HeatGeo on three embedding-reliant task families. For text classification, we report macro-F1 on BANKING77~\citep{casanueva2020efficient}, Emotion~\citep{saravia2018carer}, and TweetEval-Sentiment~\citep{barbieri2020tweeteval}. For pair classification, we report average precision (AP) on MRPC~\citep{dolan2004automatically}, SciTail~\citep{khot2018scitail}, and WiC~\citep{pilehvar2019wic}. For semantic textual similarity, we report Spearman correlation on SICK~\citep{marelli2014sick}, STS12~\citep{agirre2012semeval}, and STS-Benchmark~\citep{cer2017semeval}. Emotion, WiC, and STS-Benchmark are treated as in-domain benchmarks; the remaining datasets are treated as out-of-domain transfer benchmarks.

\paragraph{Models.}
We keep two teacher-student configurations: BGE-M3~\citep{chen2024bgem3} $\rightarrow$ BERT-base~\citep{devlin2019bert} and Qwen3-Embedding-4B~\citep{zhang2025qwen3embedding} $\rightarrow$ BERT-base. Teacher embeddings are cached once before HeatGeo training. After graph construction, the teacher model is removed from GPU memory; student training uses cached teacher embeddings, sparse diffusion targets, and spectral coordinates.

\paragraph{Training data.}
The distillation corpus is constructed from unlabeled text drawn from the in-domain training split following the TALAS protocol. For pairwise datasets, both sides of each pair are flattened into the text corpus before graph construction. HeatGeo builds the teacher semantic graph over this corpus and trains without using downstream labels.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare HeatGeo with the full teacher, the undistilled BERT-base student, SimCSE-unsup~\citep{gao2021simcse}, cross-tokenizer distillation methods CDM~\citep{chen2025cdm} and DSKD~\citep{zhang2024dskd}, sentence embedding distillation methods Jasper and Stella~\citep{zhang2024jasperstella} and DistillCSE~\citep{gao2021distilcse,xu2023distillcse}, EMO~\citep{truong2025emo}, and TALAS~\citep{dao2026talas}. Component-level comparisons against pointwise anchoring and mini-batch relational matching are included in the ablation study.

\paragraph{Evaluation protocol.}
For classification, the encoder is frozen and a logistic regression classifier is trained on top of the sentence embeddings. For pair classification and STS, examples are embedded independently and compared by cosine similarity. We report per-task scores, the average over in-domain tasks, the average over out-of-domain tasks, and the overall average across all benchmarks.

\paragraph{Implementation details.}
Unless otherwise specified, HeatGeo uses graph size $k=50$, graph temperature $\tau_g=0.1$, diffusion scales $\{1,2,4\}$, candidate size $64$, $32$ diffusion positives, $16$ hard negatives, $16$ random negatives, spectral dimension $16$, and five training epochs. The scale weights are $(1.0,0.5,0.25)$. The loss weights are $\lambda_1=1.0$, $\lambda_2=0.1$, and $\lambda_3=0.05$.
